# Code of conduct

This project is a place for learning. We expect everyone who takes part (in issues, pull
requests, discussions, and reviews) to show:

- respect and patience, especially with people who are just starting out;
- criticism aimed at the content and the code, never at the person;
- welcoming language, with no harassment, insults, sexual innuendo, or discrimination of any
  kind;
- no exposure of other people's personal data.

Behavior outside these lines may lead to removed comments, a temporary block, or a ban from the
project, at the maintainers' discretion.

To report a conduct problem without exposing anyone, use GitHub's "Report content" option on
the comment or profile, or open a private report under **Security → Report a vulnerability**
and say that it concerns conduct.

This text is based on the principles of the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1
(https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
